# Portfolio · Art & Science

> "You are here to discover the deepest truth about yourself."

The personal website of **Tanaka Hernandez, Ken (健)**:
[kentanaka3.github.io](https://kentanaka3.github.io/).

The site combines a professional portfolio and CV with a minimalist fish-school simulation. It presents my work across physics, mathematics, and computing, while inviting visitors to observe how simple interactions produce collective behavior.

## Purpose

> "The whole is more than the sum of its parts"

The website serves two complementary purposes:

- **Ingestion:** experience, research, projects, and professional background made accessible.
- **Contemplation:** a moving school of fish explores _emergence_, the formation of collective patterns through local interactions.


## Artistic approach

> "I think nature's imagination is so much greater than man's, she's never going to let us relax"

An ordered sidebar sits beside a deep-blue field of moving fish. Each fish follows limited rules, with no central choreographer directing the school. The navigation organizes a professional life into categories; the animation offers a fluid, a dynamic visual counterpart to support the idea that: a whole can exhibit behavior not apparent in any one part. Together, they connect the structure of a portfolio with the _curiosity_ of a scientific observer.

> "In seeking the meaning of life, I discovered myself instead."

The artwork invites reflection on individuality and collective behavior, observation and interpretation, consciousness and continuity. These are _questions_ to explore...
