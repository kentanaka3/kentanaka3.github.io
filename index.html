Selected Projects

Scientific Computing in Practice

Automated Seismic Data Processing Pipeline

End-to-end HPC pipeline for seismic event detection: waveform acquisition, ML-based phase picking (SeisBench), octree-based association (PyOcto), and 3D hypocenter location (NonLinLoc). Dynamic SLURM job generation with template-based configuration and GNU Make orchestration.

Event Matching & Validation Framework

Novel validation system using Hungarian algorithm-based bipartite optimization with custom weighted similarity metrics (location, magnitude, origin time). Enables rigorous comparison against authoritative catalogs (INGV). Open-source contribution for reproducible seismology research.

High-Dimensional Statistical Inference & Approximate Message Passing

Investigated rank-one estimation in spiked Wigner matrix models under Prof. Jean Barbier at ICTP. Engineered a prior-independent Onsager reaction scheduler for Approximate Message Passing (AMP), establishing recovery thresholds and analyzing fundamental algorithmic phase transitions under structured noise.

Additive Manufacturing Thermal Simulator

High-fidelity finite element simulation of powder bed fusion processes. Validated against experimental thermal imaging data from HP's Multi Jet Fusion production line.

Global Magnetization Conserved Spin Exchange Dynamics as a Social Dynamics Simulation Engine

Monte Carlo framework for studying segregation and opinion dynamics in complex networks. Implemented Ising-based models with Kawasaki-Schelling hybrid dynamics and characterized critical exponents via finite-size scaling. Research supervised by Dr. Soham Biswas.

Totally Asymmetric Simple Exclusion Process (TASEP)

Simulated established TASEP results and modeled the effects of a traffic light using Fortran and GNUPlot, under the supervision of Dr. Soham Biswas.

Local Detection of Community Structures

Introductory research during a Research Assistantship at Universidad Autónoma de Nuevo León (UANL), on local detection of community structures in complex networks under Dr. Satu Elisa Schaeffer.
