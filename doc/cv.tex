statistical inference, and large-scale scientific computing}. Proven expertise
in \textbf{HPC, Big Data, and AI} by architecting and deploying
\textbf{scalable, distributed systems} for high-throughput research and
enterprise workloads, spanning \textbf{multi-node GPU Deep Learning pipelines
on Tier-0 supercomputers (CINECA Leonardo)}.

\section*{Selected Projects}
\cvproject{Automated Seismic Data Processing Pipeline}{End-to-end HPC pipeline
  for seismic event detection: waveform acquisition, ML-based phase picking
  (SeisBench), octree-based association (PyOcto), and 3D hypocenter location
  (NonLinLoc). Dynamic SLURM job generation with template-based configuration
  and GNU Make orchestration.
}

\cvproject{Event Matching \& Validation Framework}{
  Novel validation system using \textbf{Hungarian algorithm-based bipartite
  optimization} with custom weighted similarity metrics
  (location, magnitude, origin time). Enables rigorous comparison
  against authoritative catalogs (INGV).
  Open-source contribution for reproducible seismology research.
}

\cvproject{High-Dimensional Statistical Inference \& Approximate Message
Passing}{
  Investigated rank-one estimation in spiked Wigner matrix models under
  Prof.\ Jean Barbier at ICTP. Engineered a prior-independent Onsager reaction
  scheduler for Approximate Message Passing (AMP), establishing recovery
  thresholds and analyzing fundamental algorithmic phase transitions under
  structured noise.
}

\cvproject{Additive Manufacturing Thermal Simulator}{
  High-fidelity \textbf{finite element simulation} of powder bed fusion
  processes. Validated against experimental thermal imaging data from HP's 3D
  Multi Jet Fusion production line.
}

\cvproject{Global Magnetization Conserved Spin Exchange Dynamics as a Social
Dynamics Simulation Engine}{
  \textbf{Monte Carlo framework} for studying segregation and opinion dynamics
  in complex networks. Implemented Ising-based models with Kawasaki-Schelling
  hybrid dynamics and characterized critical exponents via finite-size scaling.
  Research supervised by Dr.\ Soham Biswas.
}

\cvproject{Totally Asymmetric Simple Exclusion Process (TASEP)}{
  Simulated established TASEP results and modeled the effects of a traffic
  light using \textbf{Fortran and GNUPlot}, under the supervision of
  Dr.\ Soham Biswas.
}

\cvproject{Local Detection of Community Structures}{
  Introductory research during a Research Assistantship at Universidad
  Autónoma de Nuevo León (UANL), on local detection of community structures
  in complex networks under Dr.\ Satu Elisa Schaeffer.
}
